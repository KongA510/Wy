# 创建和分组身份组
<p class="Content_body">身份组，尤其是组身份组，在 Innovator 中有多种用途。<span style="mso-spacerun: yes;"> </span>以下是这些用途和目的的一些示例：</p>
<p class="Bulleted_list"><span style="font-family: Symbol; mso-fareast-font-family: Symbol; mso-bidi-font-family: Symbol;"><span style="mso-list: Ignore;">·<span style="font: 7.0pt 'Times New Roman';">       </span></span></span> 定义和控制用户/组的安全性，或特定组允许的权限。</p>
<p class="Bulleted_list"><span style="font-family: Symbol; mso-fareast-font-family: Symbol; mso-bidi-font-family: Symbol;"><span style="mso-list: Ignore;">·<span style="font: 7.0pt 'Times New Roman';">       </span></span></span> 决定可对条目执行的动作，如添加、更新、删除和/或</p>
<p class="Bulleted_list"><span style="font-family: Symbol; mso-fareast-font-family: Symbol; mso-bidi-font-family: Symbol;"><span style="mso-list: Ignore;">·<span style="font: 7.0pt 'Times New Roman';">       </span></span></span> 发现权限。</p>
<p class="Bulleted_list"><span style="font-family: Symbol; mso-fareast-font-family: Symbol; mso-bidi-font-family: Symbol;"><span style="mso-list: Ignore;">·<span style="font: 7.0pt 'Times New Roman';">       </span></span></span> 决定活动分配，例如工作流步骤中的审批流程。</p>
<p class="Bulleted_list"><span style="font-family: Symbol; mso-fareast-font-family: Symbol; mso-bidi-font-family: Symbol;"><span style="mso-list: Ignore;">·<span style="font: 7.0pt 'Times New Roman';">       </span></span></span> <span style="mso-fareast-font-family: Calibri; mso-bidi-language: EN-US;">可以提升生命周期状态，并可被分配给工作流活动。</span></p>

<p class="Content_body">可以使用组身份组创建多层级的层次结构访问控制。身份组的所有成员都会继承该身份组的访问权限。<span style="mso-spacerun: yes;"> </span>成员通过身份组 ItemType 中的"成员"选项卡进行分配。<span style="mso-spacerun: yes;"> </span>一个用户别名身份组可以属于多个组身份组，在这种情况下，用户的访问权限或权限将是累加的。</p>

<p class="Content_body">以下是创建新身份组并添加其他身份组作为其成员的方法：</p>
<p class="Content_body">从目录 --&gt; <span class="menuchoice"><b style="mso-bidi-font-weight: normal;">管理</b></span>，选择<span class="menuchoice"><b style="mso-bidi-font-weight: normal;">身份组</b></span>。</p>
<p class="Content_body">点击新建条目图标<img :src="'/aras-images/image715.gif'" alt="" style="border: none;" border="0"> <span style="mso-spacerun: yes;"> </span>以显示身份组表单：</p>
<p class="Content_body_indented"><img :src="'/aras-images/image713.gif'" alt="" style="border: none;" border="0"></p>

<!--?rh-list_end ?-->
<p class="Content_body">填写以下属性：</p>
<p class="Numbered"><span class="rls-2-numberedlist">a.</span><span style="font-size: 6.0pt;">         </span><span class="bold">名称</span> - 身份组的名称。<span style="mso-spacerun: yes;"> </span>这是必填项。</p>
<p class="Numbered"><span class="rls-2-numberedlist">b.</span><span style="font-size: 6.0pt;">         </span><span class="bold">描述</span> - 身份组的描述，通常用于标识目的。</p>
<p class="Numbered"><span class="rls-2-numberedlist">c.</span><span style="font-size: 6.0pt;">         </span><span class="bold">是否为别名</span> - 指示此身份组是否对应于特定的登录用户。<span style="mso-spacerun: yes;"> </span>组身份组不</p>
<p class="Numbered"><span style="mso-spacerun: yes;">       </span> 是别名。</p>
<p class="Numbered"><span class="rls-2-numberedlist">d.</span><span style="font-size: 6.0pt;">         </span><span class="bold">成员选项卡</span> - 列出此组身份组的所有成员。</p>

<p class="Content_body"><span style="font-size: 11.0pt; line-height: 107%; font-family: Calibri, sans-serif; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: Calibri; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-bidi-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-theme-font: minor-bidi; mso-ansi-language: EN-US; mso-fareast-language: EN-US; mso-bidi-language: AR-SA;">要向身份组添加成员，有两种选择——添加现有身份组，或在添加时创建新身份组。</span> <span style="mso-spacerun: yes;"> </span><span style="font-size: 11.0pt; line-height: 107%; font-family: Calibri, sans-serif; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: Calibri; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-bidi-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-theme-font: minor-bidi; mso-ansi-language: EN-US; mso-fareast-language: EN-US; mso-bidi-language: AR-SA;">每种选择都有其优势。</span> <span style="mso-spacerun: yes;"> </span><span style="font-size: 11.0pt; line-height: 107%; font-family: Calibri, sans-serif; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: Calibri; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-bidi-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-theme-font: minor-bidi; mso-ansi-language: EN-US; mso-fareast-language: EN-US; mso-bidi-language: AR-SA;">如果您正在创建新的结构，如</span>  <a href="#" class="internal-doc-link">管理用户</a>中所述，那么当场创建新的身份组是一个很好的选择。</p>

<p class="Content_body">要添加新的身份组，请使用动作下拉框中的<span class="menuchoice"><b style="mso-bidi-font-weight: normal;">创建关联</b></span>选项。</p>
<p class="Content_body"> <img :src="'/aras-images/image714.gif'" alt="" style="border: none;" border="0">。</p>

<p class="Content_body">要添加现有成员，请使用动作下拉框中的<span class="menuchoice"><b style="mso-bidi-font-weight: normal;">选取关联</b></span>选项。</p>
<p class="Content_body"><img :src="'/aras-images/image716.gif'" alt="" style="border: none;" border="0"></p>

<p class="Numbered"><span style="mso-list: Ignore;">a.</span><span style="font: 7.0pt 'Times New Roman';">     </span><span style="mso-list: l0 level1 lfo1;"> 点击动作下拉框旁边的</span> <span class="menuchoice"><b style="mso-bidi-font-weight: normal;">新建条目</b></span> <span style="mso-list: l0 level1 lfo1;">图标 <img :src="'/aras-images/image717.gif'" alt="" style="border: none;" border="0">。</span></p>
<p class="Numbered"><span style="mso-fareast-font-family: Arial;"><span style="mso-list: Ignore;">b.<span style="font: 7.0pt 'Times New Roman';">     </span></span></span> 如果是创建新的身份组，只需输入新身份组的名称。<span style="mso-spacerun: yes;"> </span>如果是添加现有的身份组，将打开一个搜索对话框。</p>
<p class="Numbered"><span style="mso-list: Ignore;">c.</span><span style="font: 7.0pt 'Times New Roman';">     </span> 选择要添加为成员的身份组，然后点击<span class="menuchoice"><b style="mso-bidi-font-weight: normal;">保存、解锁并关闭</b></span>图标<span style="mso-tab-count: 1;"> </span><img :src="'/aras-images/image718.gif'" alt="" style="border: none;" border="0">退出对话框。
