<div>
<h1>添加论坛</h1>
<p class="Content_body" style="font-weight: normal;">单击书签面板中论坛区域的<span style="font-weight: bold;">添加论坛</span>按钮，即可创建新的论坛。</p>
<p class="Content_body">要创建新论坛，请执行以下操作：</p>
<ol style="list-style: decimal;">
<li>
<p class="Numbered" style="text-indent: -36px; margin-left: 36px;">单击书签面板中的<span style="font-weight: bold;">添加论坛</span>按钮 <img :src="'/aras-images/addforbutton.jpg'">，打开<span style="font-weight: bold;">创建新论坛</span>对话框<span style="font-weight: bold;">。</span></p>
</li>
</ol>
<p class="Content_body" style="text-indent: 72px;"><img alt="" border="0" :src="'/aras-images/newforum.jpg'"></p>
<ol start="2" style="list-style: decimal;">
<li>
<p class="Numbered" style="text-indent: -36px; margin-left: 36px;">在文本框中输入论坛名称。</p>
</li>
<li>
<p class="Numbered" style="text-indent: -36px; margin-left: 36px;">按 <span style="font-weight: bold;">Enter</span> 键以创建论坛。该论坛将被<span style="font-size: 11.0pt; mso-bidi-font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: Arial, sans-serif; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-ansi-language: EN-US; mso-fareast-language: EN-US; mso-bidi-language: AR-SA;">添加到书签中，并默认对所有人可用。</span></p>
</li>
<li>
<p class="Numbered" style="text-indent: -36px; margin-left: 36px;">要对此论坛设置不同的权限或共享设置，请右键单击论坛并选择<span style="font-weight: bold;">共享...</span>。这将打开"共享论坛"对话框：</p>
</li>
</ol>


<p class="Numbered" style="font-weight: normal;"><img :src="'/aras-images/sharefor.jpg'"></p>
<ol start="5">
<li style="list-style: none; display: inline;">
<ul>
<li style="list-style: disc;">
<p class="Content_body"> <span style="font-weight: bold;">公开</span> - 使论坛对所有人可用。</p>
</li>
<li style="list-style: disc;">
<p class="Content_body"><span style="font-weight: bold;">私有</span> - 创建<span style="font-size: 11.0pt; mso-bidi-font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: Arial, sans-serif; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-ansi-language: EN-US; mso-fareast-language: EN-US; mso-bidi-language: AR-SA;">仅对创建者可见的私有论坛。</span></p>
</li>
<li style="list-style: disc;">
<p class="Content_body"><strong>与身份组共享</strong> - 创建要共享论坛的身份组列表。</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p class="Content_body"> 有关更多信息，请参阅以下章节。</p>
<h2>创建共享身份组论坛</h2>
<p class="Numbered" style="margin-left: 72px;"><span style="font-size: 11pt;">1. 在搜索框中输入身份组名称。</span></p>
</li>
</ol>
<p class="Numbered" style="text-indent: -72px;"><img :src="'/aras-images/sharedforum2.jpg'" style="cursor: nesw-resize;"></p>
<p class="Numbered" style="margin-left: 96px;">2. 选择您要共享论坛的身份组。</p>
<p class="Numbered" style="margin-left: 96px;">3. 单击<span style="font-weight: bold;">确定</span>保存更改，或单击<span style="font-weight: bold;">取消</span>退出<span style="font-weight: bold;">共享给</span>对话框。</p>
<p class="Numbered" style="margin-left: 84px; text-indent: -60px;">4. 在<span style="font-weight: bold;">共享论坛</span>对话框中单击<span style="font-weight: bold;">确定</span>保存更改，或单击<span style="font-weight: bold;">取消</span>退出此对话框。</p>

</div>
