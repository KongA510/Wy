# 书签
<p class="Content_body" style="font-weight: normal;">书签代表您正在关注的社交内容。所选书签的讨论内容将显示在讨论面板中。</p>

<p class="Figure_indented"><img :src="'/aras-images/bookmarks.jpg'"></p>

list-style: rh-list;" ?-->

<!--?rh-list_end ?-->
## 默认书签
<p class="Content_body">默认书签是自动设置的，对所有用户可用：</p>
<ul style="list-style: disc; font-weight: normal;">
<li style="font-weight: normal;">
<p class="Bulleted_list"><b style="mso-bidi-font-weight: normal;">所有书签：</b><span style="mso-spacerun: yes;"> </span>这是默认书签，当您打开<span style="font-weight: bold;">我的讨论</span>时处于活动状态。<span style="mso-spacerun: yes;">它会同时显示来自所有书签的汇总内容。</span></p>
</li>
<li style="font-weight: normal;">
<p class="Bulleted_list"><b style="mso-bidi-font-weight: normal;">我的消息：</b><span style="mso-spacerun: yes;"> </span>您创建的所有消息（不包括由用户间接创建的历史消息）</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p class="Content_body" style="mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; font-weight: normal; mso-ansi-language: EN-US; mso-fareast-language: EN-US;"><span style="mso-bidi-language: AR-SA;">这些书签始终以固定顺序显示在顶部，且不能被折叠。</span></p>
## 书签组
<p class="Content_body"><span style="mso-ansi-language: EN-US; mso-fareast-language: EN-US; mso-bidi-language: AR-SA;">用</span>户可以创建/添加的书签被分为 4 个书签组：</p>
<ul style="list-style: disc;">
<li>
<p class="Bulleted_list"><b style="mso-bidi-font-weight: normal;"><span style="mso-bidi-font-weight: normal;">人员</span>：</b><span style="mso-spacerun: yes;"> </span>代表指定人员创建的所有消息的书签（不包括由用户间接创建的历史消息）</p>
</li>
<li>
<p class="Bulleted_list"><b style="mso-bidi-font-weight: normal;">论坛：</b><span style="mso-spacerun: yes;"> </span>代表论坛的书签</p>
</li>
<li>
<p class="Bulleted_list" style="font-size: 11.0pt; font-family: Arial, sans-serif;"><span style="mso-bidi-font-size: 12.0pt; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-ansi-language: EN-US; mso-fareast-language: EN-US; mso-bidi-language: AR-SA;"><b style="mso-bidi-font-weight: normal;">条目：</b></span>            <span style="mso-bidi-font-size: 12.0pt; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-ansi-language: EN-US; mso-fareast-language: EN-US; mso-bidi-language: AR-SA; mso-spacerun: yes;"> </span>            <span style="mso-bidi-font-size: 12.0pt; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-ansi-language: EN-US; mso-fareast-language: EN-US; mso-bidi-language: AR-SA;">代表单个条目的书签</span></p>
</li>
</ul>
<p class="Bulleted_list" style="font-size: 11.0pt; font-family: Arial, sans-serif;"><span style="mso-bidi-font-size: 12.0pt; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-ansi-language: EN-US; mso-fareast-language: EN-US; mso-bidi-language: AR-SA;">每</span>个组在书签中都有一个标题，可以展开或折叠该组。</p>
## 书签搜索
<p class="Content_body"><span style="font-weight: bold;">搜索书签</span>字段位于书签面板的顶部。在此字段中输入搜索条件，然后单击图标。搜索是通过在书签名称中搜索输入的搜索字符串来完成的。</p>
<p class="Figure_indented"><img :src="'/aras-images/searchbook.jpg'"></p>
<!--?rh-list_start level="1" an="1" class="rl-p-Figure_title" style="list-style: rh-list;
list-style: rh-list;" ?-->

<!--?rh-list_end ?-->
<p class="Content_body">当搜索结果生效时，讨论面板中仅显示符合搜索条件的书签。</p>
